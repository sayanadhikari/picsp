# 11 Photoemission and shadowing

**Feature:** photoemission from sunlit surface panels (current density J_ph cos θ), shadowing
by sphere tracing towards the sun, electrically connected objects (`group`), emitted species.

A panel with a mast in a tenuous plasma charges positive until most photoelectrons return. The
surface map of the emitted current density shows the shadow cast by the mast.

## Result

The connected panel and mast charge positive and approach about +3 V (2.6 V after 35 µs),
where the photoelectrons that escape balance the collected ambient electrons; the low-energy
photoelectrons form a cloud around the sunlit side. The surface rendering shows the region of the
panel shaded by the mast, where no photoelectrons are emitted.
