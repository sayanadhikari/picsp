# 10 Secondary electron emission

**Feature:** secondary electron emission with the Sternglass yield law
δ(E) = 7.4 δ_max (E/E_max) exp(−2√(E/E_max)), emission into a dedicated species with a
flux-weighted half-Maxwellian, several floating conductors.

Two identical isolated plates sit in a plasma crossed by a 100 eV electron beam. The plate
without SEE keeps charging negatively to repel the beam. On the plate with δ_max = 1.6 the
emitted secondaries outnumber the collected beam electrons, part of the secondaries return, and
the plate floats close to the plasma potential with the current balance closed.

## Result

Final potentials −45 V (no SEE, still charging after 1 µs) and −0.4 V (with SEE). The current
balance of the SEE plate: 0.49 mA beam, 0.15 mA plasma electrons and 0.23 mA returning
secondaries collected against 0.87 mA emitted, net zero within noise.
