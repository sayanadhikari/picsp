# 08 Wake behind a sphere in flowing plasma

**Feature:** drifting ion population, open boundaries injecting the one-sided flux of a drifting
Maxwellian (exact inverse-CDF sampling), floating object in a flow.

Ions stream at three times the ion sound speed past a floating sphere. A rarefied wake forms
downstream, filled in by ion focusing towards the axis further behind the body. The
animation shows the wake developing.

## Result

An ion void forms directly behind the floating sphere, followed by an ion-focusing region with
about twice the ambient ion density and a weakly positive wake potential.
